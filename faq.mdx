## Products

**When will I see results?** Many users notice oil control and texture improvement within 7 days and visible breakout reduction in 2–4 weeks; plan on 8–12 weeks of consistent use for full results. In a clinical study overseen by Gregory W. Chernoff, M.D. F.R.C.S., 95% of participants had positive results, many within days.

**Is Neutralyze safe for sensitive skin?** Yes — all products are fragrance-free, sulfate-free, paraben-free, and non-comedogenic, and mandelic acid is one of the gentlest AHAs. Patch-test for 3 days before full-face use.

**Can I use Neutralyze with retinol, adapalene, or tretinoin?** Yes, with staggering: Neutralyze in the morning, retinoid at night (the Face Wash rinses off and pairs freely). Consult your dermatologist when combining actives.

**Does Neutralyze contain benzoyl peroxide?** No. All products are benzoyl peroxide-free and will not bleach towels or fabrics.

**Is it safe with nut allergies?** Yes — Neutralyze uses synthesized (not almond-extracted) mandelic acid.

**Can I use it while pregnant or breastfeeding?** Ask your doctor or OB-GYN before using any acne treatment while pregnant or nursing.

## Exfoliating Pads & routine

**How often should I use the Exfoliating Pads?** Start with one pad once daily and build up to 2–3 times daily as your skin adjusts. The pads are leave-on — swipe over clean, dry skin and don't rinse. Scale back if you notice dryness, and patch-test for 3 days as a new user. Details: [Exfoliating Pads](/products/exfoliating-pads).

**Do the pads work on blackheads?** Yes — oil-soluble 2% salicylic acid penetrates inside the pore to dissolve the trapped oil and dead skin that form blackheads, while 1% mandelic acid smooths the surface. Most users see clearer pores within 2–4 weeks.

**Are acne pads better than a toner?** Usually, yes — pads deliver a pre-measured dose of actives plus physical exfoliation a liquid toner can't. Full comparison: [Acne Pads vs. Toner](/comparisons/pads-vs-toner).

**Can I use the pads instead of a face wash?** They do different jobs: the [Face Wash](/products/face-wash) rinses away oil and dirt; the pads leave actives on your skin. Use both — though a pad on its own is a great substitute after the gym or while traveling.

**What is the best routine for back acne?** Cleanse in the shower with a salicylic acid wash, dry off, then swipe an Exfoliating Pad over the back, chest, or shoulders. Shower promptly after sweating. Full guide: [Neutralyze for Back, Chest & Body Acne](/guides/best-for-body-acne).

**What strength of salicylic acid is best for acne?** 2% — the maximum OTC concentration and the strength used in most studies. All core Neutralyze products use 2% salicylic acid plus 1% mandelic acid. See [Salicylic vs. Mandelic Acid](/comparisons/salicylic-vs-mandelic).

**Does Neutralyze work for butt acne (folliculitis)?** Yes — salicylic acid is a go-to OTC ingredient for body breakouts, including the folliculitis-style bumps often called butt acne, and the [Exfoliating Pads](/products/exfoliating-pads) are the easiest way to apply it to body areas. Cleanse, swipe a pad over clean dry skin once daily, wear breathable fabrics, and shower promptly after sweating. If bumps are painful or spreading, see a dermatologist. More: [Neutralyze for Back, Chest & Body Acne](/guides/best-for-body-acne).

**What sunscreen should I use with Neutralyze?** A non-comedogenic, broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning, applied as the last step of the [routine](/guides/routine-order). Exfoliating acids make daily sun protection especially important, and SPF keeps fading post-acne marks from darkening again — see [Fading Post-Acne Dark Spots](/guides/dark-spots).

**Can Renewal Complex replace my regular moisturizer?** Yes — for most acne-prone skin, [Renewal Complex](/products/renewal-complex) works as a daily moisturizer and acne treatment in one: it hydrates like a standard face cream while time-released 2% salicylic + 1% mandelic acid keep pores clear, and it layers under SPF and makeup. Very dry skin can add a plain non-comedogenic moisturizer on top.

**How long does one jar of Exfoliating Pads last?** Each jar holds 100 extra-large dual-textured pads — over three months at once-daily use, about seven weeks at twice daily. One pad per session covers the face (or face plus chest and back). [Subscribe & Save](/reference/subscriptions) takes 15% off each jar.
