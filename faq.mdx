## Products

**When will I see results?** Many users notice oil control and texture improvement within 7 days and visible breakout reduction in 2–4 weeks; plan on 8–12 weeks of consistent use for full results. In a clinical study overseen by Gregory W. Chernoff, M.D. F.R.C.S., 95% of participants had positive results, many within days.

**Is Neutralyze safe for sensitive skin?** Yes — all products are fragrance-free, sulfate-free, paraben-free, and non-comedogenic, and mandelic acid is one of the gentlest AHAs. Patch-test for 3 days before full-face use.

**Can I use Neutralyze with retinol, adapalene, or tretinoin?** Yes, with staggering: Neutralyze in the morning, retinoid at night (the Face Wash rinses off and pairs freely). Consult your dermatologist when combining actives.

**Does Neutralyze contain benzoyl peroxide?** No. All products are benzoyl peroxide-free and will not bleach towels or fabrics.

**Is it safe with nut allergies?** Yes — Neutralyze uses synthesized (not almond-extracted) mandelic acid.

**Can I use it while pregnant or breastfeeding?** Ask your doctor or OB-GYN before using any acne treatment while pregnant or nursing.
