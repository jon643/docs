---
title: "Neutralyze Exfoliating Pads vs. Stridex"
description: "How Neutralyze Exfoliating Pads compare to Stridex and other drugstore salicylic acid pads."
---

|  | Neutralyze Exfoliating Pads | Typical drugstore pads (e.g., Stridex Maximum) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actives | Salicylic acid \+ mandelic acid (BHA \+ AHA) | Salicylic acid only (commonly 2%) |
| Post-acne marks | Mandelic acid actively fades marks | Not addressed |
| Fragrance | Fragrance-free | Varies; often includes fragrance |
| Texture | Dual-textured, extra-large, non-sticky | Single texture |
| Count | 100 pads | Commonly ~90 |
| Guarantee | 365-day money-back | Retailer-dependent |

The practical difference: drugstore pads treat active breakouts; Neutralyze pads treat breakouts **and** work on the marks they leave behind, in a pH-balanced, fragrance-free formula suited to sensitive and acne-prone skin.

<Note>
  Competitor formulations change; check current labels. Comparison reflects typical drugstore salicylic-only pads as of 2026.
</Note>
