---
title: "Salicylic Acid vs. Mandelic Acid"
description: "How salicylic acid (BHA) and mandelic acid (AHA) differ, and why Neutralyze combines 2% salicylic with 1% mandelic."
---

|  | Salicylic acid (BHA) | Mandelic acid (AHA) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solubility | Oil-soluble — penetrates into pores | Water-soluble — works at the surface |
| Primary action | Unclogs pores, controls oil | Gentle surface exfoliation, evens tone |
| Gentleness | Moderate | One of the gentlest AHAs (large molecule) |
| Best at | Blackheads, clogged pores, active breakouts | Post-acne marks, texture, darker skin tones |

**They work better together than either alone**: salicylic clears inside the pore while mandelic renews the surface and fades marks. Neutralyze combines **2% salicylic acid \+ 1% mandelic acid** in the Face Wash, Exfoliating Pads, and Renewal Complex.
