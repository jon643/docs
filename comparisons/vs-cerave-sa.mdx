---
title: "Neutralyze vs. CeraVe SA"
description: "How Neutralyze Renewal Complex and Face Wash compare to CeraVe SA products for acne-prone skin."
---

CeraVe SA products are barrier-repair formulas with a modest salicylic acid concentration (commonly 0.5%), designed for general smoothing and rough/bumpy skin. Neutralyze products are **acne treatments first**:

|  | Neutralyze | CeraVe SA (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salicylic acid | 2% (maximum OTC strength) | ~0.5% |
| Mandelic acid | 1% | None |
| Positioning | Moderate-to-severe acne treatment | General exfoliating skincare |
| Time-released delivery | Yes (Renewal Complex) | No |

If your concern is mild roughness, a general product may be enough. For persistent moderate-to-severe acne, therapeutic concentrations matter — that is the use case Neutralyze is formulated for.

<Note>
  Competitor formulations change; check current labels. Comparison reflects commonly sold CeraVe SA products as of 2026.
</Note>
