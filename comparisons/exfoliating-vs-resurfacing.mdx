Choose [Exfoliating Pads](/products/exfoliating-pads) if your main concern is acne and breakouts; choose [Resurfacing Pads](/products/resurfacing-pads) if your main concern is rough texture, enlarged pores, fine lines, or lingering post-acne marks. They share the same dual-textured 100-pad format but carry different acids for different jobs.

## Why the acids matter

Salicylic acid is oil-soluble, so it works **inside the pore**, dissolving the oil and dead-skin plugs behind breakouts and blackheads - that is what makes the Exfoliating Pads an acne treatment. Glycolic acid is the smallest, fastest-acting AHA and works **at the surface**, lifting dull, rough skin quickly - that is what makes the Resurfacing Pads a texture and tone treatment. Mandelic acid sits in between: an AHA like glycolic, but with a much larger molecule that penetrates slowly, so it exfoliates with far less irritation (see [Mandelic vs. Glycolic](/comparisons/mandelic-vs-glycolic)).

## Using both

Many people run both: Exfoliating Pads as the everyday step, Resurfacing Pads 1-2 nights a week for texture and tone - **never both on the same night**. On Resurfacing nights, the glycolic pad simply takes the Exfoliating Pad's slot in the [routine order](/guides/routine-order). A salicylic cleanser like the [Acne Face Wash](https://www.neutralyze.com/products/neutralyze-acne-face-wash) rinses off, so it pairs with either pad. If irritation appears, cut back frequency - and since both are exfoliating acids, daily SPF is non-negotiable (see [fading post-acne dark spots](/guides/dark-spots)).
