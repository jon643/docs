**For acne-prone skin, treatment pads are usually the better choice: each pad delivers a consistent, pre-measured dose of exfoliating acids, and the textured pad adds gentle physical exfoliation a liquid toner can't provide.**

## The difference

A liquid toner is poured onto a cotton round (or patted on by hand), so the dose varies with every use. A treatment pad is pre-soaked, so every application delivers the same amount of active ingredients. [Neutralyze Exfoliating Pads](https://www.neutralyze.com/products/neutralyze-exfoliating-pads) are dual-textured and pre-soaked in a low-pH 2% salicylic acid \+ 1% mandelic acid formula — effectively an exfoliating toner and an acne treatment in one swipe.

## When pads win

- **Consistency** — the same dose of actives, every time
- **Body breakouts** — easy to swipe on the chest, back, and shoulders (see the [body acne guide](/guides/best-for-body-acne))
- **On-the-go** — after the gym, travel, sports; no bottle and cotton rounds
- **Texture** — the dual-textured pad physically lifts dead skin while the acids work

## When a toner still makes sense

Hydrating, alcohol-free toners (with no exfoliating acids) can be layered for moisture without over-exfoliating. If you use Neutralyze pads, you don't need a separate exfoliating toner — doubling up on acids risks irritation.

## How often to use pads

Start once daily and build to 2–3 times daily as your skin adjusts. Pads are leave-on — do not rinse. If you see dryness or peeling, scale back. New users should patch-test for 3 days first. Full directions: [Exfoliating Pads documentation](/products/exfoliating-pads).
