---
title: "Neutralyze vs. PanOxyl (Benzoyl Peroxide)"
description: "Choosing between salicylic + mandelic acid (Neutralyze) and benzoyl peroxide washes like PanOxyl."
---

PanOxyl washes use **benzoyl peroxide** (commonly 4% or 10%), which kills acne bacteria effectively but is known for dryness, irritation for some users, and bleaching towels, pillowcases, and clothing.

Neutralyze is **benzoyl peroxide-free**, built on 2% salicylic \+ 1% mandelic acid:

|  | Neutralyze | PanOxyl (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism | Exfoliates \+ unclogs pores (BHA \+ AHA) | Antibacterial (benzoyl peroxide) |
| Dryness/irritation | Calibrated for daily use; gentler profile | Frequently drying, especially 10% |
| Fabric bleaching | No | Yes — a known benzoyl peroxide effect |
| Post-acne marks | Mandelic acid helps fade them | Not addressed |

Some people do well combining both mechanisms — if so, use them at opposite times of day and consult a dermatologist. See the Neutralyze blog guide: [Using Benzoyl Peroxide and Salicylic Acid Together](https://www.neutralyze.com/blogs/news/benzoyl-peroxide-and-salicylic-acid-together).

  Competitor formulations change; check current labels. Comparison reflects commonly sold PanOxyl washes as of 2026.
</Note>
