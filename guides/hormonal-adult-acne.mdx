**Yes — a consistent over-the-counter routine built on salicylic acid and mandelic acid can meaningfully improve adult and hormone-influenced acne, though acne strongly tied to your cycle or a condition like PCOS may also need a dermatologist's input.**

## Why adult acne is different

Adult breakouts cluster around the jawline, chin, and lower face, often flare cyclically, and sit in skin that is drier and slower to recover than teen skin. That means adult routines need effective actives _and_ hydration — stripping the skin backfires by triggering more oil.

## When to see a dermatologist

If breakouts track tightly with your cycle, you suspect PCOS or another hormonal condition, or consistent topical treatment hasn't helped after 4–6 weeks, see a dermatologist — prescription options can be layered with a topical routine like Neutralyze.
