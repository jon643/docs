## Daily routine (morning and evening)

1. **Cleanse — Acne Face Wash.** Wet skin, massage in, rinse, pat dry.
2. **Exfoliate/treat — Exfoliating Pads.** Wipe a pad over the affected area. Do not rinse. (Start once daily; increase gradually.)
3. **Moisturize/treat — Renewal Complex.** Apply a thin layer once skin is dry.
4. **Daytime only — SPF 30\+.** Salicylic and mandelic acid increase sun sensitivity; sunscreen is essential, especially while fading dark spots.
5. **Makeup** can be applied after Renewal Complex has absorbed (3–5 minutes).

## Pairing with other actives

- **Retinoids (retinol, adapalene, tretinoin):** use Neutralyze in the morning and the retinoid at night, or alternate nights. The Face Wash rinses off and pairs freely.
- **Hydrating ingredients (hyaluronic acid, niacinamide):** layer after Neutralyze steps have dried.
- **Strong vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid):** use at the opposite time of day.
- **Benzoyl peroxide:** avoid layering at the same time of day; consult a dermatologist.

<Note>
  Every Neutralyze product exfoliates. If you use all three plus other actives, watch for over-exfoliation (persistent dryness or irritation) and scale back frequency.
</Note>
