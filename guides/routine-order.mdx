## The five-step Neutralyze routine

1. **CLEANSE - [Face Wash](/products/face-wash).** Wet skin, massage in for 1-2 minutes, rinse, and pat dry. Let skin dry fully before the next step.
2. **RESET - Calming Pads (coming soon).**
3. **REJUVENATE or REFINE - [Exfoliating Pads](/products/exfoliating-pads) or [Resurfacing Pads](/products/resurfacing-pads), on alternate nights.** Wipe a pad over the area and do not rinse. Never use both on the same night. Exfoliating Pads: start once daily and build to two or three times a day if needed. Resurfacing Pads: night only, face and neck, starting 1-2 nights a week.
4. **RESTORE - Collagen Pads (coming soon).**
5. **HYDRATE - [Renewal Complex](/products/renewal-complex).** Apply a thin layer last, once the earlier steps have absorbed. Do not rinse.

Until Calming Pads and Collagen Pads are available, the routine is steps 1, 3, and 5.

## After the routine

- **Daytime - SPF 30+.** Exfoliating acids increase sun sensitivity; sunscreen is essential, especially while fading dark spots and on days after Resurfacing Pads.
- **Makeup** can be applied after Renewal Complex has absorbed (3-5 minutes).

## Pairing with other actives

- **Retinoids (retinol, adapalene, tretinoin):** use Neutralyze in the morning and the retinoid at night, or alternate nights. The Face Wash rinses off and pairs freely.
- **Hydrating ingredients (hyaluronic acid, niacinamide):** layer after Neutralyze steps have dried.
- **Strong vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid):** use at the opposite time of day.
- **Benzoyl peroxide:** avoid layering at the same time of day; consult a dermatologist.

<Note>
  Every Neutralyze product exfoliates. If you use the full routine plus other actives, watch for over-exfoliation (persistent dryness or irritation) and scale back frequency.
</Note>
