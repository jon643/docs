The best moisturizer for acne-prone skin is non-comedogenic, oil-free, and fragrance-free — and if breakouts are your main concern, a medicated moisturizer that pairs hydration with acne-fighting actives does two jobs in one step.

## Why acne-prone skin still needs a moisturizer

Skipping moisturizer is one of the most common acne mistakes. Acne treatments like salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide, and retinoids are drying; when skin gets dry and tight, it often produces *more* oil, and an irritated barrier tolerates treatment worse. A well-chosen moisturizer keeps the routine sustainable.

## The label checklist

- **Non-comedogenic** — formulated and tested not to clog pores.
- **Oil-free / lightweight** — gels, lotions, and light creams layer under SPF and makeup without adding shine.
- **Fragrance-free** — fragrance is a common irritant on acne-prone, treated skin.
- **No heavy occlusives** on breakout-prone areas (dense balms, coconut oil).
