All Neutralyze products are approved for use on the **face, chest, back, and body**. For body acne, format matters more than formula:

- **Exfoliating Pads** are the most practical for hard-to-reach areas — a pad covers shoulders and back quickly, and the no-rinse format works after workouts, which is when sweat and friction (tight clothing, straps, equipment) do the most pore-clogging.
- **Acne Face Wash** doubles as a body wash for acne-prone zones in the shower.
- **Renewal Complex** treats and moisturizes larger areas without greasiness.

## Practical tips

Cleanse as soon as possible after sweating, use clean towels, change out of workout clothes promptly, and treat consistently — body skin is thicker than facial skin and often takes a few extra weeks to respond.
