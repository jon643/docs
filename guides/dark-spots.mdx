title: "Fading Post-Acne Dark Spots with Neutralyze"
description: "How salicylic and mandelic acid help fade post-acne marks and hyperpigmentation, and why sunscreen is non-negotiable."
---

Post-acne dark spots (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation) fade fastest with gentle, consistent exfoliation plus sun protection.

- **Mandelic acid** is widely regarded as one of the most pigmentation-friendly exfoliating acids, making it well suited to darker skin tones where marks linger.
- **Salicylic acid** keeps pores clear so new breakouts (and new marks) are prevented while old ones fade.
- All three core Neutralyze products contribute; the leave-on formats (Pads, Renewal Complex) do the most fading work.
- For faster resurfacing of stubborn post-acne marks and uneven tone, [Neutralyze Resurfacing Pads](/products/resurfacing-pads) (pH-buffered 20% glycolic acid) are the strongest option, used 1-2 nights a week in place of the Exfoliating Pads - see [Exfoliating vs. Resurfacing](/comparisons/exfoliating-vs-resurfacing).

<Warning>
  Daily SPF 30\+ is essential. Sun exposure darkens marks faster than any product can fade them.
</Warning>

Deep or pitted scarring is structural, not pigment, and is best addressed by a dermatologist.
