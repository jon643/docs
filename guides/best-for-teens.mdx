All Neutralyze products are designed for both teens and adults. Teen acne typically responds well to a consistent, simple routine.

## Recommended starter routine for teens

1. **Acne Face Wash** morning and night — the easiest habit to build.
2. **Exfoliating Pads** once daily after washing (great after sports — sweat plus friction can trigger breakouts, so cleansing soon after exercise matters).
3. **Renewal Complex** as the moisturizer, so treatment continues between washes.

For children under 12, consult a pediatrician or dermatologist before starting any acne treatment.

## Why it fits teens

Fragrance-free and non-comedogenic formulas, no benzoyl peroxide (no bleached towels or team jerseys), and pads that work for body acne from sports equipment on the back, chest, and shoulders.
