## Directions

<Warning>
  New users: apply sparingly to one or two small affected areas for the first 3 days as a sensitivity test.
</Warning>

If no discomfort occurs, wipe a pad over the entire affected area one to three times daily. Do not rinse off. Start with one application daily and increase gradually. If bothersome dryness, peeling, or irritation occurs, reduce to once a day or every other day.
