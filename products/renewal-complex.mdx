## Full ingredients (INCI)

## Directions

<Warning>
  New users: apply sparingly to one or two small affected areas for the first 3 days as a sensitivity test.
</Warning>

Apply a thin layer to the entire affected area one to three times daily; do not rinse off. Absorbs quickly, so SPF and makeup can be layered on top. Start with one application daily and increase gradually; reduce frequency if dryness or irritation occurs.

## Best for

The moisturizer step of an acne routine — it treats while it hydrates, replacing a separate moisturizer. Not a retinoid: many users pair it with a nighttime retinoid, using Renewal Complex in the morning. See the [routine guide](/guides/routine-order).
