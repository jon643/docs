## Neutralyze Face Wash (5 oz)

Water, Sodium C14-16 Olefin Sulfonate, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Salicylic Acid, Acrylates Copolymer, Sodium Chloride, Mandelic Acid, Phenoxyethanol, Sodium Hydroxide, Glycerin, Glycol Distearate, Laureth-4, Tetrasodium Glutamate Diacetate, Ethylhexylglycerin, Sorbic Acid

## Neutralyze Exfoliating Pads (100 ct)

Water (Aqua), Alcohol, Cocamidopropyl Dimethylamine, Arginine, Salicylic Acid, Betaine, Sarcosine, Mandelic Acid

## Neutralyze Renewal Complex (3.21 fl oz / 95 mL)

Water (Aqua), PPG-15 Stearyl Ether, Glycerin, Salicylic Acid, Polyacrylate Crosspolymer-6, Mandelic Acid, Sarcosine, Betaine, Phenoxyethanol, Arginine, Tetrasodium Glutamate Diacetate, T-Butyl Alcohol

## Neutralyze Resurfacing Pads (100 ct)

Water (Aqua), Glycolic Acid, Ammonium Hydroxide, Sodium Polyaspartate, Betaine, Benzyl Alcohol, Sodium Hyaluronate, 1,2-Hexanediol, Caprylyl Glycol

## Notes

- Face Wash, Exfoliating Pads, and Renewal Complex contain **2% salicylic acid (BHA)** and **1% mandelic acid (AHA)** - time-released in Renewal Complex.
- Resurfacing Pads contain **20% glycolic acid (AHA)**, pH-buffered, with sodium hyaluronate (hyaluronic acid).
- **Mandelic acid is synthesized**, not extracted from almonds — safe for people with nut allergies.
- All products are non-comedogenic, fragrance-free, paraben-free, and cruelty-free (never tested on animals).
- Manufactured in the USA in an FDA-registered, cGMP-certified facility; products pass RIPT skin-sensitivity, stability, and regulatory OTC testing.
